Legal

Privacy Policy

Effective October 25, 2026

This policy describes how the Kaia Mori Publisher application ("the app") handles information. The app is operated by Kaia Mori for her own TikTok account, @itskaiamori.

What the app does

The app is used solely to publish Kaia Mori's own content to her own TikTok account, with her authorization, via TikTok's official API.

What the app stores

The app stores only the OAuth access token and refresh token for that one TikTok account (@itskaiamori), so it can post on her behalf without repeated logins. It stores no other credentials and no content beyond what is being published.

What the app does not do

  • It collects no data from TikTok users or viewers — no profiles, comments, messages, or analytics about anyone else.
  • It sells nothing — no data is sold, rented, or traded, ever.
  • It shares nothing with third parties — the tokens are used only to communicate with TikTok's official API.

Revoking access

Access can be withdrawn at any time: the account owner can revoke the app's tokens in TikTok's settings, which immediately stops the app from publishing.
